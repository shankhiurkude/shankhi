About
IT YNR

Pioneering technology consulting and digital innovation since 2016.

IT YNR is an Information Technology consulting company established in 2016 in London, United Kingdom. Since its inception, it has positioned itself as a pioneering, independent, and privately held organization offering consulting services across various sectors of technology and digital innovation.

The company operates across multiple global locations, with a presence in the UAE, Netherlands, USA, Canada, and India.

IT YNR is a leading provider of software business consulting, with strong expertise in SAP S/4 HANA modules including EWM, IBP, PP-PI, PP, Repetitive Manufacturing, Quality Management, SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC), ABAP, BASIS, and more. The company also specializes in SAP S/4 HANA integrations and interfaces with external systems such as Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES), Central Control Rooms, and Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS).

Leveraging best practices developed over years of experience in the IT industry, IT YNR is committed to delivering high-quality consulting, process re-engineering, implementation, and support services. The goal is to help clients maximize the value of their IT investments and achieve optimal business outcomes.

The company has built a strong track record with a wide base of satisfied customers.
