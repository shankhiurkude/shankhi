Our Group Companies

IT YNR, Parth Infotech, APX Technology, PN Automation

We are supported by a strong network of specialized companies, enabling us to deliver end-to-end solutions across IT, SAP, automation, and IoT.

APX Technology

Focused on smart monitoring and IoT-based solutions, APX Technology develops systems that help businesses and agriculture sectors make better, data-driven decisions.

Key offerings:
  • Smart Weather Stations and environmental monitoring systems
  • Soil moisture and precision agriculture solutions
  • IoT-based automation and real-time data tracking

Their solutions are designed to improve efficiency, reduce manual effort, and support smart and sustainable operations.

Parth Infotech

Established in 2000, Parth Infotech is a global IT solutions provider offering enterprise software, SAP services, and custom development solutions.

Expertise:
  • SAP S/4HANA and enterprise solutions
  • Custom software, web, and mobile application development
  • Cloud solutions (AWS, Azure, GCP) and system integration
  • IoT-based agriculture solutions and precision irrigation

They deliver scalable, cost-effective, and customized IT solutions to clients across industries worldwide.

Pn Automation

Pn Automation is a technology-driven company specializing in software development, automation, and digital transformation solutions.

Capabilities:
  • Custom software and mobile application development
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI) and automation solutions
  • Cloud computing, cybersecurity, and system integration
  • Data-driven solutions for enterprise and government projects

They focus on building scalable, secure, and intelligent systems that turn data into actionable insights and business outcomes.
